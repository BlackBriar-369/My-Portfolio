Research

Selected research project and conference work.

UCICS 2026 · Poster PID 139

Comparative Analysis of Machine Learning Classifiers Using SMOTE Balancing for Credit Card Fraud Detection

A comparative study of ten machine learning classifiers on a highly imbalanced Kaggle credit card transaction dataset. The work used SMOTE balancing and evaluated models with accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, PR-AUC, ROC-AUC, and confusion-matrix metrics.

  • Compared ensemble, linear, instance-based, neural, and probabilistic classifiers.
  • Found that accuracy alone is misleading for severe class imbalance.
